## LinkedIn (EN)

"AI will replace senior developers."

I hear it every week. What I see in real projects is the opposite.

AI writes code faster than anyone. But every line still has to be reviewed, and the cost of a wrong decision grows with the speed.

AI won't ask:
→ what happens when the load grows 100x
→ what if an external service goes down
→ who maintains this in six months
→ what happens to the data

That is the engineer's job. So I'd skip chasing every new AI tool and strengthen the foundation: architecture, databases, Git, Docker, networking, debugging, security.

AI gives you speed. Direction still comes from the engineer.
